5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

The export’s pages overlap, so the same report can appear twice; the table counts each report id once. The intake tag (“tagged as”) is a guess from the reporter’s browser locale and is wrong often: it is not the cause. The cause follows the tenant: their locale, and the booking form they run.

The two problems

parser. The shared date parser in sched-prod reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking on 7 April goes out as 4 July. Day and month swap whenever the day is 1 to 12. It hits every channel: app, email and form. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads the value the tenant’s way, day first. Same symptom, different bug: it only hits booking-v3 tenants in day-first locales, and only through the form. The parser fix does not touch it. Until now it had no ticket of its own.

Read differently: the one-day moves

A few reports in the parser group describe a one-day shift, not a month flip: a Tuesday that showed as Monday, the same meeting on different days in Lisbon and Toronto, an invite that arrived before the booking. The desk noted them in desk-notes.json. By the kb’s rule they are counted where they are filed, by tenant, and footnoted under the table above. Engineering should check them against the parser fix: a one-day move points at time zones, not at the parser.

For the desk

  • Subject line alone is not enough. Before merging a dates report, look up the tenant’s form: booking-v3 plus a day-first locale is hint, everything else day-first is parser. The dedupe explorer runs this check.
  • Keep the counts in components. The old page typed “412 reports” into a paragraph; the number in the table above is computed and will follow the data when the desk keeps working.
  • Decision 0001 accepted that same bugs end up in separate tickets. #4471 shows the other failure: different bugs in one ticket. A new decision record should replace it.
